Reflection and Further Planning

Where to start: Start here if you have already implemented activities to boost engagement and want to reflect and plan what comes next. The steps can be used in order or on their own.

1. Purpose

Answer the five open-ended questions in the teacher reflection questionnaire. Compare your initial and follow-up evaluations to identify strategies to maintain, adapt, or further develop. Finally, use the activity-planning template to design one additional or adapted activity that addresses the needs identified and supports students' engagement.

2. Instructions

  1. Answer the five open-ended questions in the teacher reflection questionnaire.
  2. Compare your initial and follow-up evaluations.
  3. Identify strategies to maintain, adapt, or further develop.
  4. Use the activity-planning template to design one additional or adapted activity that addresses the needs identified.
