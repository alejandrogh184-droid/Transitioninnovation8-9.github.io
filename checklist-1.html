Initial checklist

Objective: To assess teacher observations of 9th grade students’ engagement patterns across academic expectations, social relationships and teaching methodologies prior to implementing targeted engagement activities.
Rating scale
1 = Never / 2 = Rarely / 3 = Sometimes / 4 = Often / 5 = Always

Academic expectations

1. Students respond to the learning goals and required outcomes for 9th grade English.
2. Students show willingness to invest effort in completing the classroom tasks.
3. Students perceive English tasks relevant to their personal interests.

Social relationships

1. Students interact with peers during pair or group work.
2. Students collaborate with classmates to complete shared language tasks.
3. Students participate voluntarily, ask questions or share ideas aloud.

Teaching methodologies

1. Students follow explanations and instructions when autonomous or communicative tasks are assigned.
2. Students start adapting to the methodological expectations of 9th grade.
3. Students show active interest when communicative teaching strategies are introduced.
