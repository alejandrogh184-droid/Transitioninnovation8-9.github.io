What it is

The Innovation Plan Toolbox is a set of practical tools for English teachers working with students who move from 8º básico to 1º medio. Each tool has a clear purpose, simple steps and a concrete result.

Use them in order to build a full innovation plan, or pick the one you need for the challenge you have this week.

How it works

The toolbox has 9 classroom activities, each with its own page, and 2 evaluation checklists to reflect on how they worked.

Pick an activity, try it with your students and use the checklists to evaluate it.

The why behind it

The move from primary to secondary school is a delicate moment for English learning. Students face more demanding content, a new school routine and, often, a drop in motivation and confidence.

Many teachers want to innovate but have little time and no shared method. This toolbox gives EFL teachers a common language and small, realistic steps to make this transition smoother for their students.

What we aim for

  • Close the gap between 8º básico and 1º medio.
  • Keep students motivated and speaking English.
  • Decide with evidence from your own classroom.
  • Share what works with other teachers.

Share your practice

Did you try one of these tools in your classroom? Tell us what you did, what you observed and what you concluded. Your experience can help other EFL teachers.

It takes about 5 minutes. Please do not include students' names or personal data.

Open the form

The form asks for

  • The tool you used and the course level.
  • Your checklist or steps.
  • Your observations.
  • Your conclusions.
  • The strategy you used and whether you would repeat it.
