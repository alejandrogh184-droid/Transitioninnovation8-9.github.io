Step 3 – Follow-up Evaluation

Optional step: Use the follow-up checklist if you want to reflect on how your students responded to the activities. You can skip this step and go directly to Step 4. The steps can be used in order or on their own.

1. Purpose

Complete the follow-up checklist after implementing the selected activities. Evaluate students' responses by identifying positive changes, negative changes, or aspects that remained unchanged across the three dimensions of engagement.

2. Instructions

  1. Implement the selected activities in your lessons.
  2. Complete the follow-up checklist after implementing them.
  3. Evaluate students' responses across the three dimensions: academic expectations, social relationships and teaching methodologies.
  4. Note positive changes, negative changes, and aspects that remained unchanged.
