Activity Selection and Implementation

Where to start: Start here if you already know which aspects of engagement you want to improve. The steps can be used in order or on their own.

1. Purpose

Review the nine classroom cases and identify those that best represent your students' needs. Select and adapt the corresponding engagement-focused activities, then implement them in your EFL lessons.

2. Instructions

  1. Review the nine classroom cases.
  2. Identify the cases that best represent your students' needs.
  3. Select and adapt the corresponding engagement-focused activities.
  4. Implement them in your EFL lessons.
  5. Optional: share your experience in the Share your practice section.
