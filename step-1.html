Initial Identification

Where to start: Start here if you want to identify your students’ initial engagement patterns before choosing any activity. The steps can be used in order or on their own.

1. Purpose

Complete the initial checklist based on your observations of students' engagement, considering academic expectations, teaching methodologies, and social relationships.

2. Instructions

  1. Complete the initial checklist based on your classroom observations.
  2. Consider academic expectations, teaching methodologies, and social relationships.
  3. Use your observations to identify the situations that require attention.
