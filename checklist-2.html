Follow-up checklist

Objective: To monitor students' engagement levels through observable behaviours following the implementation of engagement-focused activities in EFL lessons.
Rating scale
1 = Never / 2 = Rarely / 3 = Sometimes / 4 = Often / 5 = Always

Academic expectations

1. Students completed the tasks according to the established learning objectives.
2. Students showed active effort throughout the task.
3. Students remained focused on achieving the activity’s learning goals.

Social relationships

1. The students interacted respectfully during the activity.
2. The students worked collaboratively with their classmates to complete the activity.
3. The students participated, asked questions and shared ideas during the activity.

Teaching methodologies

1. Students followed the instructions and procedures provided for the activity.
2. Students independently carried out the different stages of the activity after receiving the instructions.
3. Students engaged with the materials and resources provided during the activity.
