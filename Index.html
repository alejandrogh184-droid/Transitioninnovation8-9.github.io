What it is

The Innovation Plan Toolbox is a set of practical tools for English teachers working with students who move from 8º básico to 1º medio. Each tool has a clear purpose, simple steps and a concrete result.

Use them in order to build a full innovation plan, or pick the one you need for the challenge you have this week.

How it works

The tools are grouped into four phases: Discover what your students need, Ideate new classroom strategies, Test them with small experiments and Scale what works.

Choose a phase, open a tool and work on your own or with your department.

The why behind it

The move from primary to secondary school is a delicate moment for English learning. Students face more demanding content, a new school routine and, often, a drop in motivation and confidence.

Many teachers want to innovate but have little time and no shared method. This toolbox gives EFL teachers a common language and small, realistic steps to make this transition smoother for their students.

What we aim for

  • Close the gap between 8º básico and 1º medio.
  • Keep students motivated and speaking English.
  • Decide with evidence from your own classroom.
  • Share what works with other teachers.

The tools

Filter by phase and tap a tool to see its description.

Student voice survey

Discover · 30 min

A short survey to learn how your students feel about English, what they enjoy and where they feel stuck.

Transition gap map

Discover · 60 min

Compare skills and content from 8º básico with what 1º medio expects, and spot the gaps to work on.

Activity brainstorm + SCAMPER

Ideate · 60 min

Generate classroom activity ideas and adapt your current ones using seven guiding questions.

Backward lesson planner

Ideate · 90 min

Start from the learning goal, define how you will know students reached it, then design the lessons.

Mini-pilot lesson

Test · 1 class

Try a new activity with one course for a single class and collect quick feedback from students.

Experiment matrix

Test · 45 min

Write your hypothesis, how you will measure it and what counts as success before you try a new strategy.

Reflection log

Scale · 15 min/week

Record what worked, what did not and what you will change, so your learning is not lost.

Innovation roadmap

Scale · 2 h

Order your initiatives by impact and effort and set milestones for each term of the school year.

Share your practice

Did you try one of these tools in your classroom? Tell us what you did, what you observed and what you concluded. Your experience can help other EFL teachers.

It takes about 5 minutes. Please do not include students' names or personal data.

Open the form

The form asks for

  • The tool you used and the course level.
  • Your checklist or steps.
  • Your observations.
  • Your conclusions.
  • The strategy you used and whether you would repeat it.
